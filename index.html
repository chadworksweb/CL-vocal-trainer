A pitch trainer for artists, driven by your own stems. Everything runs on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Step 1 of 3

Load your vocal stem

Export the lead vocal from your session and load it here. It becomes the melody line you train against.

Any audio file works; WAV gives the cleanest read. Use an isolated vocal, since a full mix makes the target line muddy. On a phone, wear headphones so the guide does not leak into your mic.

Step 2 of 3

Add a backing track

Optional. Load an instrumental to sing over. Without one, the vocal stem itself plays as your guide.

Step 3 of 3

Set your guide volume

This is how loud the reference plays in your ears. Keep it up while you learn a part, then pull it down to test yourself.

The line and the dot

The blue line is the target melody. Your green dot is your voice; keep it riding on the line.

green = in tune yellow = close red = off

Enable your mic

The trainer listens so it can score your pitch. Your voice is analyzed on your device and never uploaded.
