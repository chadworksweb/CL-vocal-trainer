Sing along, hit the line.

A pitch trainer for artists, driven by your own stems. Everything runs on your device; nothing is uploaded.

  1. Load a vocal stem (WAV) exported from your session.
  2. Optionally load a backing track to sing over (otherwise the vocal plays as a guide; turn Guide down to test yourself).
  3. Tap Enable mic, then Play.

The blue line is the target melody. Your green dot should ride on top of it.

Works best with an isolated vocal. A full mix makes the target line muddy. On a phone, use headphones so the guide does not leak into your mic.
